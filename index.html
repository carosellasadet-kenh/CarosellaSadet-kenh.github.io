YT Auto Upload

YT Auto Upload is a personal desktop tool for macOS that uploads and schedules videos on YouTube channels managed by its owner. It reads finished videos, thumbnails, subtitles and SEO text files from local folders on the owner's computer and uses the YouTube Data API to upload each video as private, set its title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions and playlist, and schedule its publication time.

The tool is used only by its owner for their own channels. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts.
